# Juno Bank - Product Description

## Overview

Juno is a premium bank designed for highly personalized service to corporates and high net worth individuals (HNWI). The website serves as the digital front door, reflecting the bank's commitment to excellence, exclusivity, and tailored financial solutions.

## Target Audience

### Primary: High Net Worth Individuals (HNWI)

- Net worth: $1M+
- Looking for personalized wealth management
- Value privacy and discretion
- Expect premium, white-glove service

### Secondary: Corporate Clients

- Medium to large enterprises
- Need tailored corporate banking solutions
- Require dedicated relationship managers
- Value efficiency and expertise

## Brand Identity

### Tone & Voice

- Sophisticated and professional
- Trustworthy and confident
- Exclusive but approachable
- Clear and concise

### Visual Style

- Minimalist and elegant
- Premium feel with generous whitespace
- Subtle animations for polish
- High-quality imagery

### Design Principles

1. **Clarity** - Information hierarchy is immediately clear
2. **Elegance** - Every element serves a purpose
3. **Trust** - Visual cues that reinforce security and reliability
4. **Premium** - Details that signal quality and exclusivity
